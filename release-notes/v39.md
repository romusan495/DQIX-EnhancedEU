DQIX Enhanced EU v39: right brightness with Windows HDR, dialogues at full speed, your keyboard key on the X Menu sign.

**What's new since v38**
- **Normal brightness in fullscreen with Windows HDR** (View menu, on by default): with HDR on in Windows, fullscreen showed the game far too bright. When HDR is detected on the game's monitor, fullscreen now follows Windows' "SDR content brightness" like a normal window. Without HDR, nothing changes.
- **Your keyboard key on the "X Menu" sign**: keyboard players see their own key drawn in the game's own pixel style, on the game's button, with the character it types on your layout (AZERTY shows A, é...). Long names (Esc, PgUp, Ctrl...) widen the key, and they are translated (Échap, Entf, Canc, Supr...). The sign hides under the game's menus. Controller hints are unchanged.

**Fixed since v38**
- Dialogues with a name plate dropped to about 35 frames per second with the Modern window style. They are back to full speed, with the same picture.

Thanks to Tsu for the keyboard key.

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or European). **Already on v31.x or later?** The game offers the update when it starts (or Help > Check for updates): your saves and settings are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you update to v39.
