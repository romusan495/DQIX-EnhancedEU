DQIX Enhanced EU v36: character creation on one screen, any screen shape, modern item cards.

**What's new since v35**
- **Character creation on one screen**: the touch screen's choices (all 8 steps, the name's keyboard and the name) pop up as windows beside your character, instead of both screens side by side. Clicks on them work as on the touch screen.
- **Type the name with the keyboard** (Modern > Type the character's name with the keyboard, off by default): each letter you type presses the game's own key for it, accents and signs included. Backspace erases, Enter is OK.
- **Widescreen fits any screen shape**: the picture follows your window's or monitor's shape, so 21:9 screens have no black bars, and the window opens with your monitor's shape. Menus with a 3D model over a page (Equip, the adventure log) fill their sides with their page blurred and dimmed instead of streaks.
- **Modern item cards and shops**: an item's card (buying, Equip) is a small modern window in the top left corner, its rarity as a light word with gold and grey stars. A shop's list shows at once, with icons the sign's size and clean lines between the list and the panels.
- **Battle screen for a party of one**: a lone hero now gets the same battle layout as a full party.

**Fixed since v35**
- The Journal no longer opens before an adventure is loaded (a J in the name opened it).
- An item's picture over the field (found in a chest or on the ground) has smooth edges.
- Name plates: the PC letters sit at the right height, and the plate's frame has no see-through row. A dialogue window's corner no longer leaves a pixel outside its line.
- HD text: letters placed before the font's rows were known are drawn again in the right place.
- Widescreen menus: long item names in Equip no longer show on the blurred side.

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or European). **Already on v31.x or later?** The game offers the update when it starts (or Help > Check for updates): your saves and settings are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you update to v36.
