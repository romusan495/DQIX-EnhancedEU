DQIX Enhanced EU v31.8: battle lists stay with the commands.

**Fixed since v31.7**
- In battle, opening **Spells**, **Items** or **Abilities** showed the whole
  commands screen in the top right corner. The list now stays in the bottom
  left with the current member's commands, as on the DS, and the other
  party panels stay in their row along the top.

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and
double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or
European). **Already on v31.x?** The game offers the update when it starts
(or Help > Check for updates): your saves and settings are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the
game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you
update to v31.8.
