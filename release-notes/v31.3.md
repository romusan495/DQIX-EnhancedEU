DQIX Enhanced EU v31.3: ready-to-play defaults, a simpler menu, fuller bug reports.

**What's new since v31.2**
- **New defaults**: a new install now starts the way the project is played:
  Enhanced, dynamic widescreen with the map in its own window, the battle
  HUD, map markers, smoothed and xBR textures, thin outlines, Minimal
  windows and the Marcellus text font.
- **Updating from an older version**: your display settings are reset to
  these new defaults once. Everything else is kept: volume, language,
  wireless, your name, the gold farm, full screen and the second window's
  position.
- **Simpler menus**: options most players never need are hidden (Classic,
  fixed 3D resolutions, the Screen menu, the other layouts, the small
  screen's corner/size/opacity, the other fonts...). Nothing was removed:
  set `all_options = 1` in `settings.ini` to see them all again.
- **Fixed**: with a widescreen layout in Classic mode, the commands screen
  showed huge and the battle small in a corner. Classic now shows the
  screens stacked (your widescreen choice comes back with Enhanced).
- **Bug reports (F10)** now hold the whole window as you saw it
  (`window.bmp`), your `settings.ini` and `controls.ini`, and which ROM you
  play (USA or Europe), so display problems can be found from the report.

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and
double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or
European). **Already on v31.1 or v31.2?** The game offers the update when it
starts (or Help > Check for updates): your saves are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the
game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you
update to v31.3.
