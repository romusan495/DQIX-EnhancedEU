DQIX Enhanced EU v31.2: colour job icons and a new battle layout.

**What's new since v31.1**
- **Modern job icons**: with Graphics > HD textures on, the job badges next
  to each party member in battles are smooth colour icons (a white symbol
  on a badge in the job's colour) instead of the DS's small grey pictures.
  They are made from your own ROM when the game is built; if that step
  fails, the game simply shows its own badges.
- **Widescreen battle layout**: the party's panels are stacked at half size
  in the bottom left, the active member's panel (with the commands) on top.
  The **Experience Earned** and **Attributes Increased** windows at the end
  of a battle now show big at the top in the middle instead of tiny in the
  corner. Works with both window styles (Like the DS and Minimal).
- HD texture packs can now replace 2D sprites too, not only 3D textures
  (dumps of the sprites go to `textures/dump-2d`).

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and
double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or
European). **Already on v31.1?** The game offers the update when it starts
(or Help > Check for updates): your saves and settings are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the
game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you
update to v31.2.
