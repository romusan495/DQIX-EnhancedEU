DQIX Enhanced EU v32: a modern quest sheet, smooth HD textures, whole name plates.

From this release on, versions are whole numbers: v32, v33 and so on. The game's update check knows that v32 comes after v31.11.

**What's new since v31.11**
- **Quest sheet** redrawn as one modern window: light title, the description and the date, grey lines. The **Complete!** stamp is drawn again in HD, with its slam, and the sheet steps aside while the hero shows the trick learnt.
- **HD textures: Real-ESRGAN's picture as it is**, the smooth painted look, instead of half mixed with a sharp enlargement. Packs made the earlier way are put aside in `textures-methode5` and made again, once, the first time `textures-hd` runs after the update (textures without an original are kept). `TEXHD_SHARP=<0..100>` still mixes the sharp version in. Textures with nothing to draw (all see-through or one colour) get no HD version.

**Fixed since v31.11**
- HD text: name plates are whole in the PC font (they were cut to their middle rows, with the game's letters showing under them), and their panel reaches the grey line.
- Modern windows: no shading left between the line and the panel, and no filled corner past the line when a name plate sits over the dialogue window.
- HD sprites stay on their own screen: the party's cards at the bottom of the top screen no longer spill over the field in the single window, and HD sprites are only drawn where the sprite shows.

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or European). **Already on v31.x?** The game offers the update when it starts (or Help > Check for updates): your saves and settings are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you update to v32.
