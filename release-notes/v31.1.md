DQIX Enhanced EU v31.1: the European ROM, your language, and a lot more Enhanced.

This is a fork of [sackchief/dqix-enhanced](https://github.com/sackchief/dqix-enhanced)
v31 with the European version supported and new options on top. Like the
original, it contains no game: you build it from your own cartridge's ROM.

**What's new since v31**
- **European ROM** (Dragon Quest IX, Europe, En/Fr/De/Es/It): it now builds
  and plays. **Language** menu (English, French, German, Italian, Spanish),
  saved in `settings.ini`. The built-in Wi-Fi server serves the European
  version too (not tested yet). The USA ROM works as before.
- **Graphics** menu (Enhanced mode):
  - **HD text** with a real PC font, in the menus and now in the dialogue
    windows too. **Text font**: nine fonts come with the game (Lora, Crimson
    Pro, Alegreya, Spectral, EB Garamond, Philosopher, Marcellus, Nunito,
    Fredoka) plus five Windows ones.
  - **Upscaled textures, xBR** for the 3D and **Smooth 2D** (menus, maps,
    sprites).
  - **HD texture packs**: dump the game's textures as PNGs and load your own
    from the `textures` folder. `textures-hd.exe` makes them with
    Real-ESRGAN, half mixed with a sharp enlargement of the original so hair
    and eyes stay crisp.
  - **HD movies from the movies folder**: plays movies you upscaled
    yourself (scripts in `tools/`).
  - **Character outlines**: DS, Thin or None.
  - **Window style**: Like the DS (default) or **Minimal** (no frame, a more
    see-through panel) for the menus, the dialogue box and the widescreen
    battle HUD (see-through panels in each member's colour over the 3D).
- **Widescreen battles**: the battle panels are drawn over the 3D on one
  screen, the party along the top and the active member with the commands
  bottom left; mouse clicks on them work like the touch screen. With the
  small screen in its own window, that window keeps showing the map during
  battles. Modern > Widescreen: battle HUD over the 3D (on by default).
- **Map markers** (Modern menu) on the top screen's map, or the map window
  in widescreen:
  - every **chest, pot and barrel** of the place, greyed once emptied;
  - **bookshelves** with their book's title (recipe books in purple);
  - **quest givers**: a yellow "!" when a quest is offered, a blue "…" while
    it is under way, grey once done;
  - each **building's door** gets a badge listing what its rooms hold.
  Hover a marker with the mouse to see its contents, in the game's language.
  A second item also marks the ones already emptied or done (greyed).
- **Farm gold** (Modern menu, **Ctrl+G**): an automatic gold farm.
- PlayStation button names in Game > Controls for DualSense/DualShock pads.

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and
double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or
European). **Coming from sackchief's version?** Install this one in its own
folder and copy your saves over. From now on, Help > Check for updates
follows this repository's releases.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the
game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version.
