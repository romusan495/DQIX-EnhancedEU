DQIX Enhanced EU v31.4: a map in the corner, faster battles and a Cheats menu.

**What's new since v31.3**
- **Map in the top right corner**: in widescreen the map (with its chest,
  bookshelf and quest markers) now sits small in the top right corner of the
  one game window; the second window is gone. It disappears during battles
  and fades while one of the game's menus is under it. **M** on the keyboard
  or the **right stick click (R3)** shows or hides it (changeable in
  Game > Controls); Modern > Map size: small, medium or large. The field's
  "X Menu" sign moves to the bottom right.
- **Screen layouts removed**: the other layouts, single screen, swap and the
  small screen's corner/size/opacity/own window are gone, with their keys
  (F2, U, F9, K, I, O). Without OpenGL 3.3 the game still shows the two
  screens stacked.
- **Modern > Battle speed**: Normal, 2x or 3x. Battles and their victory
  messages run faster while the music keeps its pitch.
- **Modern > Map: show the monsters walking nearby** (red dots, on).
- **Game > Keep a copy of every save** (on): each time the game saves, the
  previous save is copied to `save-backups` (the last 20 are kept).
- **Cheats menu**: Action Replay codes for your ROM's version (European or
  USA), one check mark each, all off by default: gold, gold in the bank,
  mini medals and skill points (you choose the number), full HP/MP in
  battle, every vocation, every party trick, Quester's Rest, day/night, and
  monsters' HP to 0. Never used while playing with friends.
- **Minimal window style**: the **Experience Earned** and **Attributes
  Increased** windows and the battle text box keep a thin white outline.
- **Fixed**: the gold farm only recognised the monsters of Angel Falls'
  field; it now works everywhere.

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and
double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or
European). **Already on v31.x?** The game offers the update when it starts
(or Help > Check for updates): your saves and settings are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the
game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you
update to v31.4.
