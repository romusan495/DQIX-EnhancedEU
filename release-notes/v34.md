DQIX Enhanced EU v34: no more black trees or white shadows while HD textures are being made.

**Fixed since v33**
- While `textures-hd` was making HD textures in the background (after an update or a build), the game could pick up its half-made work pictures as HD textures: black squares round trees, white shadows, black floors. The work folder now sits outside `textures` (in `textures-hd-tmp`), the game skips the old one, and the old one is cleared the next time `textures-hd` runs.

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or European). **Already on v31.x, v32 or v33?** The game offers the update when it starts (or Help > Check for updates): your saves and settings are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you update to v34.
