DQIX Enhanced EU v31.11: chest ranks on the map, a modern battle screen, cleaner PC letters.

**What's new since v31.10**
- **Treasure-map grottoes**: each chest's mark on the map shows its rank letter (S to I), and its tooltip gives the contents, the rank and whether it was opened. Blue chests in the field show their rank too. While the treasure map you follow is open, a small box on it lists the chest ranks possible per floor.
- **Modern battle screen** (with the Modern window style): the party's cards and the commands' windows look like the other windows, see-through black with a thin grey line, instead of each member's colour. While a spell, item or target list is open, only the card of the member choosing stays.
- **PC font letters**: letters follow each other at the PC font's own spacing (no more gaps after a narrow r or i), and letters drawn inside sprites (messages like "X has no items.", name plates) use the PC font too. Out of battles.
- **HD textures of the whole game made by themselves**: if Real-ESRGAN is installed, the build takes every 3D texture out of your own ROM once and makes the HD pack in the background, without slowing the build. Nothing from the ROM is shared.

**Fixed since v31.10**
- In widescreen, the open treasure map stays in the map corner with its picture, instead of a pop-up that cut the picture away.
- The commands screen no longer shows up small in a corner at the end of a battle.
- The modern grey line goes round each window, not round a name plate and the dialogue window together.
- HD text: a battle message letter's shadow is drawn under it, with room before the next letter.

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or European). **Already on v31.x?** The game offers the update when it starts (or Help > Check for updates): your saves and settings are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you update to v31.11.
