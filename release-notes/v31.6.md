DQIX Enhanced EU v31.6: a faster start and a round of bug fixes.

**What's new since v31.5**
- **Modern > Start** (from the next launch): skip the logos (the new
  default), skip the logos and the opening movie to land straight on your
  adventure log, or keep both as on a DS.
- **Item cards**: in Items and Equipment, the item's card is no longer shown
  tiny inside the map in the corner; both screens are shown side by side
  while it is open, then the usual view comes back.

**Fixed** (reported by players, thank you!)
- Saving at a church and quitting left the screen black in widescreen.
- Specks in the level-up and attributes numbers with HD text.
- A magenta fringe around the save quill with Smooth 2D.
- The bestiary and the skill points screen: the bottom screen covered
  everything; both screens are now shown side by side.
- A one-line Experience Earned window is now shown on its own too.
- The Wi-Fi sign at the DS's edge no longer blacks out the side of the
  picture in widescreen.
- Map: door badges now show pots and barrels apart from chests (a house
  with only barrels showed a chest).

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and
double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or
European). **Already on v31.x?** The game offers the update when it starts
(or Help > Check for updates): your saves and settings are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the
game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you
update to v31.6.
