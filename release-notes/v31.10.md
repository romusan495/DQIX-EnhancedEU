DQIX Enhanced EU v31.10: an in-game options menu, the journal, your language everywhere.

**What's new since v31.9**
- **In-game options menu** (Esc, or the PS / Xbox / Home button): every
  setting of the menu bar, drawn over the game and usable with the
  controller, the keyboard or the mouse. The game pauses while it is open.
  View > Readability of the options menu: stronger contrast and three text
  sizes.
- **The PC interface in your language**: the menu bar, the windows and the
  options menu show in English, French, German, Italian or Spanish
  (Language > Menus and messages of this window; by default the game's
  language).
- **Journal** (Modern menu, **J** or L3): a panel over the game with
  - **Quests**: underway, not yet accepted and complete, with the request
    text and the game's hint only when you ask for it (it can give the
    answer away);
  - **Progress**: each member's attributes, EXP to the next level, unspent
    skill points and every skill line with what it unlocks next;
  - **Vocations**: the 12 vocations side by side, the best values in green;
  - **Alchemy**: the recipes you know, what each needs, what you hold and
    what is short, how many you can make now, with filters and a search;
  - **Items**: everything the bag and the party hold, and the recipes each
    one goes into.
  It only reads the game; nothing is changed.
- **Battle speed** (Modern menu): a new **4x**, and two options, both off by
  default: speed up only while the turn plays out (commands at the usual
  pace), and victory messages that go by themselves (but stop at a level
  up, so new skills and skill points stay yours to read).
- **Controller buttons in hints**: the field's "X Menu" sign and the options
  menu name the real buttons of the pad or keyboard you used last
  (PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo). Game > "Controller: confirm with the bottom
  button" swaps confirm and cancel (off by default).
- **Map legend** (Modern > Map: a legend of the marks) under the place's
  name, showing only the kinds of marks on the map.
- **Graphics**, both off by default:
  - **Soft shadows in corners and under furniture**;
  - **Thin dark lines round things**, so shapes stand apart from what is
    behind them (not on flames and glows).
- **Window style: Modern** (now the default): square windows, a dark
  see-through panel and a thin grey line, out of battles, for the menus,
  the dialogue window and the yes/no boxes. Your settings move to it once.
- **HD text: Source Sans 3**, now the only font in the menu and the default.
  A setting on the old default font moves to it.
- **Widescreen shops**: the chosen item's icon, big, beside its description
  in a modern-style window.

**Fixed since v31.9**
- HD text: after a few minutes, letters could turn into another text's
  letters (the dialogue under King Marthus's name plate).
- Far characters, speech bubbles and items on the ground are sharper again,
  and their pieces still meet.
- Widescreen: Attributes and Equip stay side by side; the equipment list no
  longer covers the stats sheet.
- Modern and Minimal windows: no dark strip above the quest offer's
  dialogue, and windows the game fades in stay faded.
- The menu bar no longer starts with an empty "Menu".

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and
double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or
European). **Already on v31.x?** The game offers the update when it starts
(or Help > Check for updates): your saves and settings are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the
game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you
update to v31.10.
