DQIX Enhanced EU v31.7: the battle panels back along the top, and two layout fixes.

**What's new since v31.6**
- **Battle layout**: the party's panels are back in a row along the top, as
  in the first widescreen battle layout. The current member's commands stay
  in the bottom left, and the big Experience Earned and Attributes Increased
  windows are unchanged.

**Fixed**
- While a turn played out, the whole bottom screen showed in the top right
  corner instead of the party's panels.
- After a battle, the field could be shown side by side with the map as if
  an item's card were open.

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and
double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or
European). **Already on v31.x?** The game offers the update when it starts
(or Help > Check for updates): your saves and settings are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the
game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you
update to v31.7.
