DQIX Enhanced EU v31.5: maps inside buildings, and no more quitting by accident.

**What's new since v31.4**
- **Maps inside buildings**: in houses, shops and churches the map in the
  corner now shows the room's own plan, with you (the blue dot) and the
  room's chests, pots, bookshelves and quests, instead of the town around
  it. The plan is drawn like the game's town maps: a parchment plaque with
  its walls and furniture. Back outside, the town map returns.
  Modern > "Map: a plan of the rooms inside buildings" (on).
- **Ask before quitting**: Escape (in a window), closing the window and
  Game > Exit now ask "Quit the game?" first. In full screen, Escape still
  leaves full screen first.
- **Fixed**: the map in the corner showed the logos, the opening credits and
  the title screen; it now waits until your adventure is loaded.

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and
double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or
European). **Already on v31.x?** The game offers the update when it starts
(or Help > Check for updates): your saves and settings are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the
game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you
update to v31.5.
