DQIX Enhanced EU v31.9: autosave, pop-up pages and shops on one screen.

**What's new since v31.8**
- **Save by itself every 10 minutes** (Modern menu, off by default): on the
  field, once the hero is free to walk, the game saves as it would at a
  church. **Continue** then resumes right where the party was. With
  Game > Keep a copy of every save, the previous saves are kept too.
- **Widescreen: top-screen pages pop up.** Item cards (Items, Equipment,
  shops), titles and the other pages the top screen shows now pop up in the
  middle of the big screen instead of putting both screens side by side.
  They move aside, and shrink if needed, so menus and the dialogue window
  stay visible. Menus with a 3D model on the top screen (monster list) stay
  side by side.
- **Widescreen shops on one screen**: each item shows its own icon before
  its name, and its description sits under the list.

**Fixed since v31.8**
- HD texture packs: far characters, speech bubbles and items on the ground
  are made of small pieces that were upscaled one by one, so heads looked
  cut and glued back. Those pieces now stay at the DS's size, smoothed.
- Shops: a pink frame could flash when going back from an item's card, and
  the minimap stayed over the Buy/Sell menu. Both are gone.
- Cutscenes: the field was sometimes shown small, side by side with a black
  screen.

**How to install**: download *Source code (zip)* below, unzip it and
double-click `Install Dragon Quest IX.bat`, then choose your own ROM (USA or
European). **Already on v31.x?** The game offers the update when it starts
(or Help > Check for updates): your saves and settings are kept.

**Not included, on purpose**: no ROM, no game executable and no data from the
game. Everything is built on your PC from the ROM you own.

**Playing with friends**: everyone needs the same version, so all of you
update to v31.9.
